# Support process

This is an internal checklist. It is not public copy. Nothing in this file was exercised against production, and no deletion or billing change was made while writing it.

## Inbox

Keep the shared Hostinger inbox. Once the first merchant enrols, the founder or a designated team member checks that inbox each business day.

There is no public response-time promise. Do not add one to the site.

Who checks the inbox is a launch task. No employee is assigned yet. Do not treat a person’s name as the owner until that assignment exists.

Mailbox delivery and the forwarding destination were not retested for this note.

## Requests

Use this list for a cancellation, a billing correction or refund, and an export or deletion request.

1. Record the request, including who sent it, when it arrived, and what they asked for.
2. Verify account ownership before any disclosure or deletion.
3. Check the approved policy. Cancellation is an email to support@cartaisy.com or the contact form before the next renewal, with access through the period already paid. Refunds are limited to billing errors, a setup fee cancelled before work starts, and refunds required by law. There is no partial refund for unused subscription time. A copy, access, correction, or deletion of personal information can be requested at any time. There is no self-serve export download in the public rule.
4. Perform the change only with existing external billing or other manual tools that are already authorized. Do not add a product permission for this.
5. Confirm the result to the person who asked.
6. Record that the request is complete.

## Gaps

- `docs/DATA_DELETION.md` describes the dashboard customer-deletion control and the unverified retention fallback. Those calls were not made.
- There is still no runbook here for deleting a merchant account, a fit-check row, or a contact message.
